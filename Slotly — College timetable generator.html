FAQ

Good questions

What if it cannot find a timetable?

It tells you why. Usually a section needs more periods than there are slots, or one teacher has too many periods. Reduce periods, add a teacher or raise the daily limit.

Can one teacher teach several sections?

Yes. Give them the same name in each row and the solver keeps them from being double-booked.

How are labs scheduled?

Labs use two back-to-back periods that stay on one side of the lunch break, at most one block per day.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser and is saved only on your device.
